About the treatment

What it is, what it does, and what it will not do.

What it is

Polynucleotides are purified fragments of DNA, extracted from salmon or trout and highly refined. They have been used in wound healing and regenerative medicine since the 1950s. The formulation we use contains polynucleotide at 2mg/ml.

Injected into the skin, polynucleotides act on your fibroblasts — the cells that produce collagen and elastin — and support hydration, repair and cell turnover. This is a regenerative treatment. It improves the quality of the skin itself rather than adding anything to sit underneath it.

How it is done

Numbing cream is applied first. The product is then placed through a series of very small, superficial injections across the under-eye area using a fine needle — a mesotherapy technique. The appointment takes around 30 minutes including numbing time.

Course and timings

  • A course of 3 treatments, 4 weeks apart, each charged separately.
  • Some improvement in hydration and brightness can be seen after the first session.
  • The fuller effect builds over the weeks following each treatment as the skin regenerates.
  • Results typically last 6 to 12 months after a completed course.
  • Maintenance is usually recommended around twice a year.
What Lumi Eyes will not do.

It is not a filler. It will not fill a hollow, a tear trough or a groove, and it will not add volume of any kind.

It will not improve eye bags caused by fat herniation. If the problem is a bulge rather than a shadow, no injectable will fix it and the honest answer is a surgical opinion.

It will not correct shadowing caused by facial structure — a deep-set eye socket or prominent orbital rim casts a shadow, and treating the skin will not change that.

It will not lift loose or crepey skin that has genuinely lost elasticity.

It will not remove pigmented dark circles caused by melanin rather than by thin skin, though skin quality improvements can make them appear a little softer.

Product and evidence

Polynucleotides as a class have a substantial history of use and a growing evidence base in aesthetic medicine. However, the specific branded formulation used here is a skin booster rather than a licensed medicine, and there is limited published trial data on this exact product as opposed to polynucleotides generally. We think it works well for the right patient, which is why we offer it — but you should know the evidence position rather than assume it is equivalent to a licensed medicine.

Polynucleotide is also not a hyaluronic acid product, which means it cannot be dissolved with hyaluronidase in the way filler can. It is broken down naturally by the body over time.

Risks and side effects

Polynucleotides are generally very well tolerated. These are set out so your decision is properly informed.

Common — expect these

  • Small raised bumps at each injection point, settling within a few hours
  • Redness immediately afterwards
  • Mild swelling for 24 to 48 hours
  • Tenderness for a day or so
  • Bruising — the skin here is thin and the area bruises easily. This can last up to two weeks.

Less common

  • Swelling that takes longer than a couple of days to settle
  • Small lumps that persist for a week or two
  • Itching at the treated area
  • Temporary pigment change at an injection point
  • Cold sore outbreak, where you are prone to them
  • Infection at an injection site, occasionally needing antibiotics
  • Less improvement than hoped, or none at all

Rare

  • Allergic or hypersensitivity reaction, including to the fish-derived component
  • A persistent nodule requiring treatment
  • Delayed inflammatory reaction, sometimes triggered by illness, dental work or vaccination weeks or months later
  • Blockage of a blood vessel by the product
  • Scarring or lasting pigment change
Because polynucleotide is not a hyaluronic acid product, it cannot be dissolved. Where a hyaluronic acid filler can be removed with an enzyme, this is broken down naturally by your body instead. It is worth knowing, though in practice the very small volumes used here mean this rarely becomes relevant.

Alternatives

You are entitled to know what else could achieve your goal, including doing nothing.

  • No treatment. Always a reasonable choice. There is no harm in declining.
  • Sleep, hydration and sun protection. Genuinely — the under-eye area responds to these more than most people expect, and no injectable will outperform chronic poor sleep.
  • Medical skincare formulated for the eye area, including retinoids and vitamin C used appropriately.
  • Other polynucleotide formulations at higher concentrations, or from different manufacturers.
  • Tear trough filler, where the problem is genuinely a hollow rather than skin quality — a different treatment with a different and higher risk profile, and not suitable for everyone.
  • PRP, using your own blood, for skin quality in the same area.
  • Skin boosters or mesotherapy, for hydration and glow.
  • Surgical opinion, where the concern is fat herniation or significant skin excess. No injectable will address either.

Aftercare

Simple, but the first day matters.

For the first 24 hours

  • Do not rub, press or massage the area. The small bumps disperse on their own.
  • No makeup, including concealer and mascara.
  • No strenuous exercise, saunas, steam rooms or very hot baths.
  • No alcohol, which increases bruising.
  • Sleep on your back with your head slightly raised if you can.
  • Avoid touching your eyes with unwashed hands.

For the first week

  • Reintroduce eye makeup only once any bumps have settled.
  • Avoid retinoids and acids around the eye area for a few days.
  • Cool compresses help if you are swollen or bruised. Never put ice directly on the skin.
  • Arnica can help with bruising.

For two weeks

  • No facials, massage, microneedling, peels or laser over the area.
  • Avoid dental treatment where possible, including the hygienist.
  • Avoid vaccinations where the timing is within your control.
  • Daily SPF, and sunglasses when it is bright.

Ongoing

  • Attend your remaining sessions as planned — the course is what produces the result.
  • Drink plenty of water and protect the area from the sun.
