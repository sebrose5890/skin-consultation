About the treatment

What it is, what it is for, and what its regulatory position is.

What it contains

  • Riboflavin — vitamin B2, included to support fat metabolism.
  • Bromelain — an enzyme from pineapple with anti-inflammatory properties.
  • A lecithin-based component — included to emulsify fat so it can be carried away and cleared by the body.

It is injected directly into the layer of fat beneath the skin, in small amounts across a grid of points, using a fine needle. Treatment takes around 20 to 30 minutes depending on the area.

Regulatory status — please read this carefully. Lemon Bottle is classified as a cosmetic product. It is not licensed by the MHRA as a medicine, and it does not hold a CE or UKCA mark as a medical device. It has not been through the clinical trial process that licensed medicines undergo, and there is no published peer-reviewed trial data of the kind that exists for some other injectable treatments.

We use it because in our clinical experience it has an excellent safety record and produces good results, and because the licensed alternatives in this category carry their own well-documented risks — see the alternatives section. But you are entitled to know its regulatory position before agreeing, and to choose differently. If you would prefer a treatment with formal regulatory certification behind it, tell us and we will discuss what else we can offer.

What it is for — and what it is not

This is not a weight loss treatment. It will not reduce your weight, and it will not work on generalised body fat. It is designed for small, stubborn pockets of fat in people who are already close to their target weight and whose weight is stable. If your goal is weight loss, this is the wrong treatment and we will discuss medically supervised options instead.

Timings

  • Swelling begins within hours and peaks over the first 2 to 3 days. In the chin and jaw area this can be pronounced.
  • Most swelling settles within 5 to 7 days, though some can persist for two weeks.
  • Results begin to show from around 3 to 4 weeks as the treated fat is cleared.
  • A course of 2 to 4 sessions, 2 to 4 weeks apart is usually needed, each charged separately.
  • Final result is assessed roughly 6 to 8 weeks after your last session.

What it will not do

  • It does not tighten skin. If skin laxity is part of the problem, removing the fat underneath may make loose skin more apparent.
  • It does not treat cellulite.
  • It does not prevent the remaining fat cells from enlarging if you gain weight afterwards.
  • It cannot achieve a surgical result.

Risks and side effects

Set out in full so you can weigh them properly.

Common — expect these

  • Significant swelling, beginning within hours, peaking at 2 to 3 days and settling over 5 to 7 days. Under the chin this can be very noticeable.
  • Redness and warmth in the treated area
  • Tenderness, aching or a bruised feeling for several days
  • Bruising, which can be extensive and last up to two weeks
  • Firmness, lumpiness or nodularity in the area as it settles, often for several weeks
  • Itching
  • Numbness or altered sensation in the treated area, usually temporary

Less common

  • Prolonged swelling or firmness lasting more than a few weeks
  • Uneven or asymmetrical result, needing further treatment to correct
  • Over-treatment producing a hollow or dented appearance
  • Loose or crepey skin becoming more apparent once fat is reduced
  • Infection at an injection site, occasionally needing antibiotics
  • Temporary pigment change at the treated site
  • Feeling unwell, headachy or fatigued for a day or two afterwards
  • Little or no visible result

Rare

  • Allergic or hypersensitivity reaction, including to bromelain
  • Persistent nodules requiring treatment
  • Abscess or deep infection
  • Damage to a nerve causing lasting altered sensation or, in the jaw area, temporary weakness of the lower lip
  • Skin damage or ulceration at the treated site
  • Scarring
Because this product is not a licensed medicine, its safety profile is not characterised in the way a licensed product's would be. That means there may be risks that are not yet documented, and that the frequency of the risks listed above is based on clinical experience rather than trial data. We are telling you this so that your decision is properly informed.

Alternatives

You are entitled to know what else could achieve your goal, including doing nothing.

  • No treatment. Always a reasonable choice. There is no harm in declining.
  • Diet and exercise. Genuinely the first line for anything other than a small, localised, stubborn pocket. Fat dissolving is not a substitute and never will be.
  • Deoxycholic acid products such as Aqualyx. These hold CE marking and have published peer-reviewed evidence behind them, which Lemon Bottle does not. However, they work by dissolving cell membranes directly, which is a more aggressive mechanism — they are associated with more pain, more swelling, and documented risks of skin necrosis and nerve injury. This is the trade-off, and it is the reason we have chosen the product we use. We are happy to discuss it fully.
  • Medically supervised weight management. Where the underlying goal is weight loss rather than contouring a specific pocket, this is the appropriate route and we offer it.
  • Skin tightening and collagen treatments. Where laxity rather than fat is the real issue.
  • Surgery — liposuction. The only method that reliably removes larger volumes of fat, with the recovery, cost and risk that comes with an operation.

Aftercare

What you do afterwards affects both your comfort and your result.

For the first 24 to 48 hours

  • Expect swelling. Cold compresses help — never put ice directly on the skin.
  • No strenuous exercise, saunas, steam rooms, sunbeds or very hot baths.
  • No alcohol.
  • Do not apply makeup or fake tan over the treated area.
  • Sleep with your head slightly raised if the chin or jaw was treated.

Massage and compression

Follow the specific massage instructions your practitioner gives you on the day. Gentle massage helps the treated fat disperse and clear, and reduces the chance of lumpiness. If compression garments have been recommended for a body area, wear them as advised.

For the following weeks

  • Drink plenty of water. The fat released has to be cleared by your body, and hydration helps.
  • Keep active with gentle movement and walking once the initial soreness settles.
  • Keep your diet and weight stable — gaining weight will work against the result.
  • Attend your remaining sessions as planned.
  • Do not judge the result before 6 weeks.

Contact us if

  • Redness is spreading, or the area is hot with discharge
  • You develop a temperature or feel generally unwell
  • Skin looks white, dusky, or begins to break down
  • You have difficulty swallowing or breathing after treatment near the chin
  • Lumps are still present after several weeks
  • Anything at all concerns you
