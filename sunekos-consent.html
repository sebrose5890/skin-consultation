About the treatment

What Sunekos is, and why the amino acids are the point.

What it is

Sunekos is made by Professional Dietetics in Italy. It combines non-cross-linked hyaluronic acid with a patented complex of six specific amino acids — glycine, L-proline, L-leucine, L-lysine, L-valine and L-alanine.

Those amino acids are the building blocks your body uses to make collagen and elastin. Hyaluronic acid on its own hydrates and can prompt some fibroblast activity; the amino acids give your cells the raw material to actually build new tissue, and they also slow the breakdown of the hyaluronic acid so it stays active in the skin for longer.

The result is regeneration of the extracellular matrix — the scaffolding your skin sits in. Sunekos has been shown to prompt production of collagen and elastin in the proportions your own skin naturally has, rather than simply increasing collagen alone.

Sunekos is not a filler. It adds no structural volume and will not fill a hollow, a line or a groove, lift descended tissue, or sharpen a contour. What it changes is the quality of the skin — hydration, elasticity, firmness and texture. If volume or structure is what concerns you, a different treatment is appropriate and we will say so.

The formulations we use

  • Sunekos Performa — the upgraded replacement for Sunekos 200. Low molecular weight hyaluronic acid with the six amino acids, in a stronger ratio than the original. Used across the face, neck, décolletage, hands and the delicate eye area, and well suited to early ageing and skin quality.
  • Sunekos 1200 — higher molecular weight hyaluronic acid with the same amino acids. Creates a hydrating "cushion" effect and is used for more advanced skin ageing, often as a first step before a course of Performa.
  • Sunekos Cell — formulated for cellulite and body skin quality.

Your practitioner selects the formulation, or combination, appropriate for your skin and the area being treated.

How it is done

Numbing cream can be applied first if you would like it. The product is placed through a series of small injections along the tension lines of the skin, at multiple depths, using either a fine needle or a cannula depending on the area. The solution is thin and injects easily, so treatment is quick and generally well tolerated.

Course and timings

  • A course of 3 sessions, around 2 weeks apart, each charged separately.
  • First visible improvement is often noticeable around 10 days after the first session.
  • The full effect builds progressively across the course and for several weeks after it.
  • A single maintenance session every 6 to 9 months preserves and builds on the result.
  • The course must be completed for the treated area to achieve the intended result — stopping partway will not give you what is described here.

Risks and side effects

Sunekos is very well tolerated. These are set out so your decision is properly informed.

Common — expect these

  • Small raised bumps at each injection point. These absorb quickly — usually within a few hours rather than days.
  • Redness immediately afterwards, generally settling within a day or two
  • Mild swelling
  • Tenderness for a day or so
  • Bruising, more likely around the eyes, on the neck and on the hands, and fading over one to two weeks

Less common

  • Swelling that takes longer than a couple of days to settle
  • Small lumps that persist for a week or two
  • Itching at the treated area
  • Temporary pigment change at an injection point
  • Cold sore outbreak, where you are prone to them
  • Infection at an injection site, occasionally needing antibiotics
  • Less improvement than hoped, or none at all

Rare

  • Allergic or hypersensitivity reaction
  • A persistent nodule requiring treatment
  • Delayed inflammatory reaction, sometimes triggered by illness, dental work or vaccination weeks or months later
  • Blockage of a blood vessel by the product
  • Scarring or lasting pigment change
A note on allergy. You may see claims elsewhere that an allergic reaction to this treatment is impossible because hyaluronic acid and amino acids occur naturally in the body. That is not accurate, and we would rather tell you so. Reactions are genuinely rare, but no injectable can be guaranteed free of them.
Contact the clinic promptly on 0800 772 0137 if you develop: pain that is severe or worsening rather than settling; skin that goes white, blotchy, mottled or dusky; spreading redness, heat or discharge; a temperature or feeling generally unwell; any change to your vision if you have been treated near the eyes; or a lump that has not settled after two weeks. If you have visual symptoms, go to A&E and tell them you have had an injection near the eye — then call us.

Alternatives

You are entitled to know what else could achieve your goal, including doing nothing.

  • No treatment. Always a reasonable choice. There is no harm in declining.
  • Medical skincare and daily sun protection, which underpin every other treatment.
  • Polynucleotides, which regenerate by a different mechanism using purified DNA fragments, and are often used alongside or instead of Sunekos.
  • Profhilo, which bio-remodels using hyaluronic acid alone, with fewer sessions but without the amino acid component.
  • PRP, using your own blood, which works on similar regenerative principles.
  • Medical microneedling, with or without regenerative add-ons, particularly for texture and scarring.
  • Collagen biostimulators such as Sculptra or Juvelook, where volume and structure matter as well as skin quality.
  • Dermal filler, where the real concern is a hollow, a line or lost structure. Sunekos will not do this.
  • Anti-wrinkle injections, where the concern is lines caused by movement.

Aftercare

Straightforward, and downtime is minimal.

For the first 24 hours

  • Do not rub, press or massage the area. The small bumps absorb on their own.
  • No makeup over the treated area.
  • No strenuous exercise, saunas, steam rooms or very hot baths.
  • No alcohol, which increases bruising.
  • Sleep on your back with your head slightly raised if you can.
  • Avoid touching the area with unwashed hands.

For the first week

  • Avoid retinoids and acids over the treated area for a few days.
  • Cool compresses help with swelling or bruising. Never put ice directly on the skin.
  • Arnica can help with bruising.
  • Drink plenty of water — hyaluronic acid works by holding it.

For two weeks

  • No facials, massage, microneedling, peels or laser over the area.
  • Avoid dental treatment where possible, including the hygienist.
  • Avoid vaccinations where the timing is within your control.
  • Daily SPF, and avoid sunbeds and deliberate sun exposure.

Ongoing

  • Attend all three sessions — the course is what produces the result, and a partial course will not.
  • Protect the area from the sun. New collagen and elastin are worth looking after.
