About the treatment

What Profhilo is, and why it is not a filler.

What it is

Profhilo is pure hyaluronic acid — the same substance your skin already produces to hold water and maintain its structure. It is made by IBSA using a patented process called NAHYCO®, in which high and low molecular weight hyaluronic acid are bonded together using heat rather than chemicals.

This matters. Conventional dermal fillers are cross-linked using a chemical agent called BDDE to make them thick enough to hold a shape. Profhilo contains no BDDE and no chemical cross-linker at all. It stays fluid, spreads through the tissue rather than sitting where it is placed, and works by stimulating your skin rather than filling it.

What it does

The low molecular weight component stimulates your fibroblasts to produce new collagen and elastin. The high molecular weight component draws and holds water, giving deep hydration. The result is an improvement in skin quality, firmness, hydration and texture — often described as looking well rather than looking treated.

Profhilo is not a filler and will not do what a filler does. It does not add structural volume, it does not lift or sharpen a jawline or cheekbone, and it will not fill a line or a hollow. If those are your goals, filler is the appropriate treatment and we should discuss that instead. Patients who arrive expecting a filler result from Profhilo are the ones most likely to feel it did not work.

The range

  • Profhilo — the original. A 2ml syringe containing 64mg of hyaluronic acid, 32mg of each molecular weight. Injected into the deep dermis of the face, neck, décolletage or hands.
  • Profhilo Structura — a more concentrated formulation designed to work in the superficial fat pads of the midface rather than the dermis, supporting the tissue that gives the face its natural contour. Often relevant where volume has been lost through ageing or weight loss, including on weight management medication.
  • Profhilo Body — a 3ml syringe containing 96mg of hyaluronic acid, for skin laxity and crepiness on the body, particularly inner arms, abdomen and above the knees.

How it is injected

Profhilo uses the BAP technique — Bio Aesthetic Points. Rather than many small injections across the face, the product is placed as five precise deposits on each side, chosen because they are anatomically safe and allow the product to spread evenly. It means fewer needle entries and a shorter appointment. The same principle is adapted for the neck, décolletage and body.

You will have visible raised bumps immediately afterwards. This is expected. Because the product is deposited as small pools rather than spread thinly, you will see and feel a lump at each injection point. These settle as the product disperses, usually within 24 hours and almost always within 48. This is not a complication and it is not a sign anything has gone wrong — but it does mean Profhilo is not a treatment to have on the day of an event.

Timings

  • The standard course is 2 sessions, 4 weeks apart. One session alone will not give the result.
  • Hydration is often noticeable within the first two weeks.
  • The full effect develops over 4 to 8 weeks after the second session, as the collagen and elastin response builds.
  • Results typically last around 6 months, after which a maintenance session keeps it going. Some people notice benefit for longer.

Risks and side effects

Profhilo has a very good safety record, but no injectable is without risk.

Common — expect these

  • Visible raised bumps at each injection point, settling within 24 to 48 hours
  • Redness at the injection points for a few hours
  • Tenderness for a day or two
  • Mild swelling

Less common

  • Bruising, which can last up to two weeks
  • Bumps that take longer than 48 hours to settle
  • Itching at the treated area
  • A result that is less noticeable than you hoped, particularly where skin laxity is significant
  • Cold sore outbreak, where you are prone to them
  • Infection at an injection site, occasionally needing antibiotics

Rare

  • Hypersensitivity or allergic reaction to hyaluronic acid
  • A persistent nodule requiring treatment
  • Delayed inflammatory reaction, sometimes triggered by illness, dental work or vaccination weeks or months later
  • Blockage of a blood vessel by the product, described below
  • Nerve irritation causing altered sensation, usually temporary
One genuine advantage worth knowing. Because Profhilo is pure hyaluronic acid with no chemical cross-linker, it can be dissolved with an enzyme called hyaluronidase if that ever became necessary. Biostimulators such as PDLLA or PLLA cannot be. This is one of the reasons Profhilo's safety profile compares favourably.
Blockage of a blood vessel (vascular occlusion) Any injectable placed under the skin can enter or press on a blood vessel and interrupt the blood supply to an area. This is rare with Profhilo — the BAP points are chosen specifically because they avoid major vessels — but it is a recognised risk of any injection and it needs treating within hours rather than days.

Contact the clinic immediately on 0800 772 0137 if, during or after treatment, you notice: pain that is severe, worsening, or out of proportion to what you were told to expect; skin that goes white, blotchy, mottled or dusky; a lace-like or net-like purple pattern on the skin; skin that feels cold; or any change to your vision. Out of hours, or if you have visual symptoms, go to A&E and tell them you have had a hyaluronic acid injection — then call us.

Do not wait to see if it settles overnight.

Alternatives

You are entitled to know what else could achieve your goal, including doing nothing.

  • No treatment. Always a reasonable choice. There is no harm in declining.
  • Dermal filler, where the real concern is lost volume or structure rather than skin quality. This is a different treatment with a different risk profile.
  • Collagen biostimulators such as Juvelook, Lenisna or Sculptra, which build collagen over months and last longer, but cannot be dissolved and carry a higher nodule risk.
  • Polynucleotides, which repair and improve skin quality by a different mechanism and are often used alongside or instead of Profhilo.
  • Other skin boosters and injectable hydration, delivered as multiple small injections rather than the BAP technique.
  • Microneedling, peels and resurfacing, for texture, tone and superficial lines.
  • Medical skincare and daily sun protection, which underpin everything else.
  • Surgery, where the concern is significant skin laxity that no injectable will address.

Aftercare

Straightforward, but the first 24 hours matter.

For the first 24 hours

  • Do not massage, rub or press the bumps. They need to disperse on their own. Pressing them can move the product where it should not go.
  • No makeup over the injection points.
  • No strenuous exercise, saunas, steam rooms or very hot baths.
  • No alcohol, which increases bruising and swelling.
  • Sleep on your back if you can.

For two weeks

  • No facials, massage, microneedling, peels, laser or energy-based treatments over the area.
  • Avoid dental treatment where possible, including the hygienist.
  • Avoid vaccinations where the timing is within your control.
  • Avoid sunbeds and prolonged heat.

Ongoing

  • Drink plenty of water — hyaluronic acid works by holding it.
  • Use sun protection daily.
  • Attend your second session at four weeks. The course is what produces the result.

Contact us if

  • The bumps have not settled after 48 hours
  • You have pain that is severe or getting worse rather than better
  • The skin looks white, mottled, dusky or feels cold
  • You notice any change in your vision
  • You develop signs of infection — spreading redness, heat, swelling or discharge
  • You develop a lump at any point, including weeks later
  • Anything at all concerns you
