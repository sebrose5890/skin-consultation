What a consultation is

And, just as importantly, what it is not.

All consultations at Sebastian Rose are carried out face to face, in clinic. We do not offer remote or telephone consultations. Assessing skin, anatomy and suitability properly cannot be done over a phone or a screen, and prescribing decisions should not be made without seeing you.

What will happen

  • We will talk about what is bothering you and what you are hoping for.
  • We will take a medical history and ask about medication, allergies and previous treatments.
  • We will assess your skin and the relevant anatomy in person.
  • We will explain the realistic options — which may include treatments you had not considered, or none at all.
  • We will discuss risks, costs, timescales and what a realistic result looks like for you specifically.
  • You will have time to ask questions.
A consultation does not commit you to anything. You are under no obligation to book, and there is no pressure to. If you would like time to think, that is not just acceptable — it is sensible, and we would rather you did.

We may recommend nothing

Sometimes the honest answer is that a treatment will not achieve what someone wants, that a different approach would serve them better, that they should wait, or that we are not the right place for them. We will tell you that plainly. We would rather lose a booking than take money for something that will not work.

We may decline to treat

There are circumstances where we will not proceed — where it would not be clinically safe, where expectations could not realistically be met, where a lesion needs assessing by a doctor first, or simply where something does not sit right with us. This is a clinical judgement made in your interest, and it is not negotiable.

If you may need prescribed medication

Some treatments, including anti-wrinkle injections, use prescription-only medicines. These can only be prescribed after a face-to-face consultation with the prescriber, and a prescription is never a formality — if you are not suitable, it will not be issued. Where a consultation is required in advance of a treatment appointment, it must be completed at least 48 hours beforehand.

Treatment on the same day

Where it is appropriate and you are ready, treatment can sometimes follow a consultation on the same visit. Separate written consent is always taken for the treatment itself, after the risks have been explained and your questions answered. If you would prefer to go away and think, say so at any point.

Fees and cancellation

Set out plainly so there are no surprises.

Consultation fees

  • Consultation fees and deposits are fully redeemable against any treatment you go on to book.
  • A charge of £10 applies where you have a consultation and are found unsuitable for treatment, or decide not to proceed.
  • Fees for treatments are discussed and agreed with you before anything goes ahead. We will never proceed with something you have not agreed to.

Cancellation

  • 48 hours' notice is required to cancel or move an appointment, in line with our terms and conditions.
  • Cancellations inside 48 hours, and non-attendance, may result in the loss of your deposit.
  • If you are unwell, tell us as early as you can — we would far rather move you than have you attend when you should not.

Please arrive prepared

  • Come with a clean face where possible if we are assessing your skin.
  • Bring a list of any medication you take.
  • If you have had treatment elsewhere, bring any records, product names or batch stickers you have.
  • If a mole or skin lesion is involved, bring your GP, dermatology or mole-check letter.
