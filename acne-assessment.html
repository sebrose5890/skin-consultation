Marks or Scars?

Most people who come to us about acne scarring don't have scarring — they have marks, which fade on their own and need something completely different. Five questions to work out which you're dealing with.

Active breakouts1 of 5

First — are you still breaking out?

This matters more than anything else, because it changes the order of treatment.

Run a finger over the area. What can you feel?

Do this in good light, in front of a mirror, without makeup.

Now the stretch test

How to do it Put a finger either side of the mark and gently stretch the skin apart. Watch what happens to it. This is the single most useful thing you can do at home, and it's what we do first in clinic too.

What colour are they?

Colour tells us a surprising amount about what's going on underneath.

How long have they been there?

Think about the ones that bother you most, not the newest.

A word of caution about this tool.
This is a guide to help you understand what you're likely dealing with — it is not a diagnosis and it cannot replace someone looking at your skin. Skin is genuinely difficult to assess from a description, most people have a combination of things going on, and the treatment that's right for you depends on a great deal this tool can't see. Please treat the result as a starting point for a conversation rather than an answer.
